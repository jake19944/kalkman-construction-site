What this work involves

Building a house is a long relationship. You'll talk to your builder more over twelve months than you talk to most friends, which is why we keep our build schedule small on purpose — you get the person who priced your job actually on your job.

We handle site prep and foundations, framing, envelope, and the full interior finish, coordinating subs and inspections along the way. You get a schedule you can follow and regular updates on what's happening next, rather than having to chase us for information.

How a build progresses

Site & foundation

Excavation, footings, foundation walls, and drainage — the part that determines whether everything above it stays square.

Framing & envelope

Structure, roof, windows, and weather barrier, getting the building closed in before the weather turns.

Mechanical rough-in

Plumbing, electrical, and HVAC run and inspected before insulation and drywall close the walls.

Interior finish

Cabinets, trim, tile, flooring, and paint — the stage where craftsmanship is most visible.
