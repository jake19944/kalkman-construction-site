What this work involves

Stock cabinets come in fixed widths, which means every installation ends in filler strips covering the space that didn't work out. Built-ins don't have that problem — they're made for the wall they're going on, floor to ceiling, wall to wall.

We build entertainment walls, window seats, mudroom lockers, bookcases, pantries, and office storage. Most of it is designed around something specific you need to store, which is a better starting point than a catalog page.
