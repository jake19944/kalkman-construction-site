What this work involves

A bathroom is the least forgiving room in a house. Everything happens in a small footprint, every surface gets wet, and shortcuts behind the tile don't show up until there's a stain on the ceiling below. The work that matters most is the work you'll never see.

We build showers on proper waterproofing systems, slope pans correctly, vent to the outside rather than into an attic, and set tile with layouts planned so cuts land where they should. Then we make it look good.

→
Waterproofing that's actually a system

Bonded membranes, sealed corners, and properly treated niches and benches. This is where cheap bathroom remodels fail first.

→
Shower pans sloped right

Water goes to the drain from every point on the floor. Sounds obvious; it's the most common defect we're called in to fix.

→
Real ventilation

A correctly sized fan ducted outside, not into the attic. Moisture control is what keeps paint, grout, and framing intact.

→
Tile layout planned first

We dry-lay and set our lines so full tiles land at eye level and cuts land where nobody's looking.

→
Accessible options

Curbless entries, blocking for future grab bars, and comfort-height fixtures if you're planning to stay in the house.

Common bathroom projects

Tub-to-shower conversion

Reclaims floor space and gets rid of the step-over. Popular with homeowners planning to age in place.

Curbless walk-in showers

A flush entry with a properly built slope. Cleanest look available, and the most demanding to build correctly.

Full gut remodels

Down to studs and subfloor, which is the only honest way to fix a bathroom with existing water damage.

Powder rooms

Small footprint, quick turnaround, and a good place to do something with a little more personality.
